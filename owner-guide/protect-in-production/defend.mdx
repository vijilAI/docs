**Defend** shows what [Dome](/concepts/platform/dome) detected in production across every
Agent.

## What Feeds Defend

Defend combines Dome detections from Agents that send telemetry to the Console with DLP
events from [Targets](/concepts/discovery/target) of type `dlp`. A `dlp` Target receives its
events from a Dome proxy. See [Create and Manage Targets](/owner-guide/discover/targets).

## What You See

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="DLP Overview" icon="chart-column">
    Counters and charts for flagged, clean, and errored DLP traffic, scoped to one or all
    `dlp` Targets.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Event Cards" icon="layer-group">
    Events grouped by Agent or device, filtered by outcome, type, direction, time range, or
    user.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Event Details" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Who sent the traffic, which app and tool it came from, what Dome did, and the text Dome
    checked.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Posture" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Open violations and alerts for your team.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Watch two numbers in particular:

- **Errored** counts events where a [Detector](/concepts/defense/detector) failed to return
  a verdict, so the traffic passed unchecked.
- **Unattributed flagged** counts detections Dome could not tie to a registered Agent. Any
  number above zero means traffic is reaching the proxy from a source missing from your
  [Agent Registry](/owner-guide/register-agents/registering-agents).

Click an event to open its details. From there you can view the whole turn or filter to
more events from the same user.
